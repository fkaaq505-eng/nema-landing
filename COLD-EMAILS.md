# Nema Cold Email Templates

Use these to reach real estate agents in your city.
Find agents on Google ("real estate agent [city]"), Zillow agent pages, or Instagram.
Send 15–20 per day. Expect 15–25% reply rate.

---

## Template 1 — The Direct Offer (highest reply rate)

**Subject:** Virtual tour for your [address] listing?

Hi [Name],

I noticed your listing at [address] — great property.

I run a service that turns standard listing photos into immersive 3D virtual tours in under 24 hours. No scheduling, no camera crew — I work from the photos you already have.

Agents using virtual tours get 3× more qualified inquiries on average.

I'd like to do one for your current listing at no charge so you can see the result. If you like it, my standard rate is $[750–1,000] per property.

Would that be useful?

[Your Name]
[Your number]

## Template 2 — The Pain Point (best for cold lists)

**Subject:** Why your listing isn't getting showings

Hi [Name],

Quick question — are you getting as many showing requests as you'd like on [property address]?

Most agents I talk to say the same thing: buyers scroll past flat photos in 3 seconds. The ones getting 2× more showings are using virtual tours.

The problem: Matterport costs $1,500+ and requires scheduling a crew.

I built a faster alternative. I take your existing listing photos and generate a full 3D interactive tour in under 24 hours. $[800] per property, delivered as a shareable link you drop straight into the MLS.

I have one free slot this week if you want to try it on your current listing.

[Your Name]

## Tips

- Personalise with the actual listing address — reply rate jumps 40%
- Send Tuesday–Thursday, 8–10am local time
- Follow up exactly once, 3 days later
- Once they say yes, deliver within 24hrs — that's your retention hook
- Ask every happy client for one referral — agents talk to each other
